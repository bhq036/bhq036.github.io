Eyelash Extension Styles

With Classic, Volume, and Hybrid lash types, we customize the design based on the shape of your eyes to create a unique look that suits both your eyes and face.

1. Classic Lashes (Natural Lashes)

  • Description: This is the basic style, where one extension is applied to each natural lash, adding length and slight volume while maintaining a natural look.
  • Best suited for: Those who prefer a natural look that’s not too dramatic.

2. Volume Lashes (Full Lashes)

  • Description: For each natural lash, 2 to 6 ultra-thin extensions are applied, creating a full and voluminous effect. The density can vary from 2D to 6D, depending on the number of extensions per natural lash.
  • Best suited for: Those looking for a bolder, more striking look; ideal for special occasions.

3. Hybrid Lashes (Mixed Lashes)

  • Description: A mix of Classic and Volume styles, where some lashes are applied individually (Classic) and others in small fans (Volume). This creates a natural yet fuller look.
  • Best suited for: Those who want moderately thick lashes that look natural yet still stand out.

4. Cat Eye Lashes

  • Description: Lashes are applied longer towards the outer corners of the eyes, creating a feline, sultry effect.
  • Best suited for: Those who prefer a bold, captivating look and want to emphasize the eyes.

5. Doll Eye Lashes

  • Description: The lashes are longer in the center, giving the eyes a rounder, doll-like appearance.
  • Best suited for: Those who love a cute, youthful style.

6. Fox Eye Lashes

  • Description: Extensions are shorter at the inner corners and gradually lengthen toward the outer corners, creating a lifted, foxy effect.
  • Best suited for: Those looking for a sharp, alluring look that makes a strong impression.

7. Wet Look Lashes

  • Description: Lashes are grouped into small clusters applied closely together, giving the appearance of freshly wet lashes with a unique, natural look.
  • Best suited for: Those who enjoy a bold, distinctive style.

8. Camellia Lashes

  • Description: Different lengths of extensions are mixed to mimic the natural lash line’s texture, providing a soft, full, and slightly unstructured appearance.
  • Best suited for: Those who prefer a naturally voluminous, gentle look.

Each lash extension style has its own charm. Carefully consider which style suits your facial features and personal preferences.
